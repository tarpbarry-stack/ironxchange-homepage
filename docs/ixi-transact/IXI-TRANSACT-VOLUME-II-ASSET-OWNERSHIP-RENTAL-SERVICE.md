# 1. ASSET ACQUISITION

Directory:

`components/ixi-aos/transact/modules/asset-acquisition/`

Asset Acquisition is the canonical opening economic chapter of an owned asset.

It must capture:

- asset / Passport identity
- seller
- purchase date
- purchase price
- invoice / bill of sale / wire evidence
- payment date / wire transfer date
- buyer premium / taxes / fees where applicable
- freight into the acquisition
- initial repairs / parts / technology / make-ready costs
- ownership partners
- ownership percentages
- ownership dollar contributions
- acquisition notes and documents

## Acquisition is not lifetime machine cost

Acquisition answers:

> What did it economically take to acquire and place this asset into service?

Lifetime machine economics may later include maintenance, additional repairs, rental income, technology, storage, revenue and disposition economics.

Do not keep inflating the Acquisition record forever.

## In-service cutover

The product uses an explicit **IN SERVICE DATE** and user confirmation.

Before cutover, qualifying costs may remain acquisition/make-ready costs according to accounting policy.

After cutover, later transactions route into ongoing asset economics.

This is intentionally simple operationally: when the company knows the machine has entered service, record the best known date and confirm it.

## Ownership structure

Ownership must support multiple partners.

Each owner interest should be able to preserve:

- owner / partner identity
- legal ownership percentage
- economic/profit share if different
- contribution amount
- effective date
- source agreement / document

Do not overwrite history when a partner later contributes more, sells out, or changes ownership.

Ownership changes are events.

# 2. OWNERSHIP CHANGES / BUYOUTS

If Partner A originally owns 20% and is later bought out, final Settlement must still be able to see:

```text
ORIGINAL OWNER
20%

BUYOUT EVENT
Date
Buyer
Amount
Transferred %
Documents

CURRENT OWNERSHIP
0%
```

Current ownership and historical ownership are different views over the same immutable event history.

A buyout payment can be economically relevant without remaining a current-owner entitlement at final Settlement.

# 3. RENTAL EXPENSE

Directory:

`components/ixi-aos/transact/modules/rental-expense/`

Rental Expense records outgoing economics when the company rents equipment/assets/services from another party.

Important facts include:

- vendor
- rented item / description
- related machine/job/location
- rental start/end
- daily/weekly/monthly rate or commercial basis
- delivery/pickup/freight
- taxes/fees
- expected vs actual total
- invoice/Bill relationships
- documents

Rental Expense should feed the same IXI Financial/A/P/Treasury/GL architecture as other outgoing economics.

# 4. RENTAL INCOME

Directory:

`components/ixi-aos/transact/modules/rental-income/`

Rental Income is incoming economics generated by company assets rented to customers.

It should retain:

- customer
- rented asset / Passport
- rental period
- rate / minimums
- meter/hour terms where relevant
- delivery/freight economics
- damage/extra-charge relationships where relevant
- invoice / payment relationships

Rental Income is not just a machine note. It must be visible to asset profitability, A/R, Treasury, GL and Reporting.

# 5. SERVICE QUOTE

Directory:

`components/ixi-aos/transact/modules/service-quote/`

Service Quote is the commercial authorization record for external customer service work.

Typical identity:

`SQ-#####`

Important data:

- customer
- customer contact
- customer asset / Passport if known
- requested problem / work
- customer-facing scope
- internal notes
- pricing type
- line items / labor / material / service
- optional/alternate scope
- estimated internal cost
- quoted revenue
- authorization limits
- quote revision
- validity / expiration
- customer PO where applicable
- accepted-by / accepted-at / acceptance method

Supported pricing doctrine:

- **ESTIMATE**
- **FIXED PRICE**
- **NOT TO EXCEED**

The accepted quote revision is preserved as a snapshot. Future work should never reconstruct authorization from whatever the quote looks like today after edits/revisions.

# 6. CUSTOMER SERVICE WORK ORDER

Directory:

`components/ixi-aos/transact/modules/customer-service-work-order/`

This layer adapts an accepted Service Quote into the canonical Work Order system.

```text
SQ-##### ACCEPTED
        ↓
Customer Service WO Adapter
        ↓
canonical Work Order contract
        ↓
CSWO-######
```

Commercial context carried into CSWO includes:

- Service Quote ID/number
- accepted revision
- customer
- customer asset / Passport
- customer PO
- pricing type
- accepted options / line snapshot
- original authorization
- approved change-order authorization
- estimated internal cost
- accepted-at / accepted-by evidence

The Work Order remains the execution engine.

Actual operational work belongs in:

- Time
- Material
- Service
- Expense
- Purchase
- Documents
- Notes
- Photos

Do not create another weak external-service work engine.

---

# 7. CHANGE ORDERS / COMMERCIAL AUTHORIZATION

A Customer Service WO can discover additional work.

Additional work does not automatically mean additional authorized revenue.

The commercial system must distinguish:

```text
ACTUAL WORK DISCOVERED
        ≠
CUSTOMER AUTHORIZATION
```

When authorization changes, preserve the accepted change-order record and update the WO commercial authorization projection.

Never silently raise an NTE ceiling because actual cost exceeded the quote.

# 8. SERVICE INVOICE — OUTGOING CUSTOMER INVOICE

Directory:

`components/ixi-aos/transact/modules/service-invoice/`

Service Invoice is the revenue-side closeout of external service work.

Typical identity:

`SINV-#####`

It should inherit rather than re-key:

- customer
- customer asset / Passport
- CSWO
- accepted SQ/revision
- customer PO
- approved change orders
- pricing type
- actual WO economics

Billing rules:

### Fixed Price
Invoice the authorized fixed commercial amount according to billing terms. Internal cost differences affect profitability, not customer authorization.

### Estimate
Actual billable work may determine the invoice while quote/estimate remains visible for comparison.

### Not To Exceed
Actual billable work may be invoiced up to the authorized ceiling. Do not exceed authorization without an approved commercial change.

Management comparison should retain:

```text
QUOTED
AUTHORIZED
ACTUAL INTERNAL COST
INVOICED
RECEIVED
```

Invoice state and A/R state are separate concepts.

# 9. SOLD / ASSET SALE

Directory:

`components/ixi-aos/transact/modules/sold/`

SOLD is a standalone sales/disposition module for the person who actually sells or disposes of the asset.

It is **not Settlement**.

The salesperson/owner/auction manager records the exit event:

- asset / Passport
- buyer
- disposition type
- sale date
- sale price
- payment terms
- due date
- deposit / expected proceeds if applicable
- buyer PO
- bill of sale number
- hours/meter snapshot where useful
- documents
- notes

Canonical identity:

`SALE-#####`

Supported disposition schema should allow:

- direct sale
- auction sale
- trade
- transfer
- total loss / insurance
- scrap
- other

The first UI can remain optimized for SALE while the contract remains capable of richer disposition types.

## Sale price is not cash received

Critical doctrine:

```text
SALE PRICE = commercial receivable / proceeds entitlement
PAYMENT = actual cash received
```

If a machine sells for $225,000 and the buyer wires $175,000:

```text
SALE PRICE       225,000
CASH RECEIVED    175,000
OPEN A/R          50,000
```

Do not mark the Sale paid merely because SOLD was recorded.

## Passport state

Recording SOLD changes the asset ownership/disposition projection but never deletes the Passport.

The Passport remains the permanent history of:

- Acquisition
- ownership
- service/work
- costs
- rental
- revenue
- sale
- settlement

---

# 10. SALE → FINANCIAL FLOW

The current architecture uses canonical financial vocabulary rather than a separate sales ledger:

```text
SALE-#####
   ↓
receivable / invoice financial truth
   ↓
COLLECTIONS / A/R
   ↓
payment(s)
   ↓
Treasury / GL / Reporting
```

The Sale record owns the disposition facts. IXI Financial owns the receivable/payment facts.
